The problem

One sensor
isn't enough

Most homes rely on manual switches or basic passive infrared (PIR) motion sensors. Both leave gaps that cost money and create risk.

TRY IT · SAME ROOM, TWO SENSORS
PIR MOTION SENSOR Occupied CORRECT

It sees the movement, so the lights stay on.

ECOSENSE Occupied CORRECT

Radar and door agree that someone is inside.

Still people get missed

PIR sensors lose track of someone who is sitting or sleeping, and can be triggered by pets, curtains or sunlight. A mmWave sensor can pick up small movement such as breathing.

Appliances keep running

Air conditioners, lights and fans left on in an empty home raise the electricity bill and add fire risk from unattended electrical appliances.

Automation needs an undo

A wrong guess should never leave you without control. EcoSense notifies you when it acts and lets you restore power from the app at any time.

How it works

Two sensors.
One decision.

An ESP32 hub reads both sensors every half second and runs the fusion on the device. The smart plug is controlled over its local HTTP API.

  1. STEP 1 · READ

    Sense

    The LD2410C mmWave radar reports presence and distance. A reed switch reports whether the door is open or closed.

  2. STEP 2 · FUSE

    Decide

    The radar is treated as evidence of presence. Door events decide how much to trust it and how long to wait.

  3. STEP 3 · ACT

    Switch off

    When occupancy confidence reaches 0, the hub sends the OFF command to the smart plug and records the power draw first.

  4. STEP 4 · REPORT

    Tell you

    The event is logged to Firebase and a notification is sent. Tap Override and the hub turns the plug back on.

RULE A

Door movement filters false alarms

Presence that appears right after the door moves is held as unconfirmed until it persists.

RULE B

Confidence counts down

When the radar stops seeing anyone, confidence falls from 100 to 0 over a configurable grace period. At 0 the room is vacant.

RULE C

A still door extends the wait

If the door did not move near the last detection, the person is probably still inside, so the countdown is extended.

Questions

Before you install

Which phones can run the app?

The app is built with Flutter and targets Android. It is shared as an APK, so you install it directly instead of from a store.

Do I need the sensor hub?

Live readings come from the EcoSense hub through Firebase. Readings appear in the app once a hub is online in your home.

What if EcoSense turns something off by mistake?

You get a notification whenever it acts, and Override in the app asks the hub to turn the plug back on.

Which appliances can it control?

Plug-in appliances connected through a smart plug, such as an air conditioner, a fan or lights.
